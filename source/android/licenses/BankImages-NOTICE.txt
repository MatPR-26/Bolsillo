Institution and service images identify accounts selected by the user. The names and marks belong to their respective owners. Bolsillo is an independent local finance app and is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by these institutions. Original PNG/JPEG files are included unchanged; publisher/source links and SHA-256 fingerprints for the new bank images are in BankImages-sources.json. Existing BancoEstado, MACHBANK, Netflix and Spotify resources retain their previous provenance. The images are bundled locally: no bank or image-host request is performed by the app.

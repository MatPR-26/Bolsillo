MaterialMotion.java adapts the createScaleAnimator and createFadeThroughAnimator
factories: original-scale restoration, property holders, fade-through progress
threshold 0.35 and final-alpha restoration. Changes: framework-only types instead
of AndroidX annotations, shorter scale/distance, centralized timing, deterministic
cancellation, bounded page targets and pressed-state feedback. No affiliation
with Google or endorsement by Material Design is claimed.

Subscription service images

The local catalogue contains service names and images for identification only.
Bolsillo is not affiliated with or endorsed by those providers. Choosing a
template does not purchase a subscription or connect an external account.

ServiceImages-sources.json records the source, version, rights metadata and
SHA-256 hash for each bundled image. Vector assets sourced through Simple Icons
retain their geometry and brand colours. SVG numeric separators are normalized
for Android VectorDrawable using svgpath 2.6.0 (MIT); parsing the result must
produce exactly the same path segments. The library's CC0 licence does
not waive trademarks or individual brand guidelines; see the bundled Simple
Icons licence and disclaimer. Publisher favicons retain their owners' rights.

Images are packaged with the app. Browsing the catalogue does not contact the
providers or download images. Prices, billing dates and account assignments
are entered by the user; no current service prices are bundled.

The original 24px Rounded SVG outlines were converted to Android VectorDrawable XML.
Original path data and proportions are unchanged. Their source viewBox origin is
preserved with a vector group translation. A neutral fill is tinted at runtime.
These assets are packaged locally; there is no icon font, network request or SVG
runtime dependency. Bank, Netflix and Spotify logos are separate existing assets.
Detailed source URLs and SHA-256 hashes: MaterialSymbols-sources.json.
